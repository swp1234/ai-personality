# AI Character Choices

Ten everyday choices produce one of eight author-created AI character metaphors. This is entertainment, not a personality assessment or an official comparison of AI products.

## Product contract

- Each accepted answer adds exactly one point to the character mapped beside that option in `js/app.js`.
- Ten accepted answers complete the activity; the first character wins a tie.
- No answer, score, or result label enters analytics or share copy.
- The seven funnel stages are exact-once: view, start, progress after five answers, complete, next click, share success, and related click.
- The result offers one primary private reflection and two related activities.
- Ad serving and traffic-expansion actions remain suspended while the invalid-traffic restriction is active.

## Local check

From the portfolio root, run `node scripts/verify-ai-personality.js`. Production uses the same verifier with `--url https://dopabrain.com/ai-personality/`.
